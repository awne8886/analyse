# review-a11y

Scope: R30, G.6, G.9, the G.23 keyboard flows, G.28 contrast, G.29 (360 px layout of all three screens), focus
order and visible focus, reduced motion for board animations, and labelled form controls.

Method: I read src/App.tsx, src/index.css and src/ui/**. I computed contrast ratios with WCAG 2.x relative
luminance from the token values in src/index.css and src/ui/theme.css. I drove the built `dist/` with
`npx vite preview --port 4191` and a standalone Playwright script in Chromium 1194, using the e2e/mocks.ts
mocks and the live game 129688175007 until `[data-testid=review][data-complete=true]`. I ran the checks at
360x780 (both themes) and at 1280x900, and repeated the board checks with `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`.

Counts: **2 high, 6 medium, 4 low**, plus optional items. The last section lists the checks that passed.

---

## High

### H1. The import screen and Settings panel are clipped at 360 px, and the R30 check cannot detect it (R30, G.29)

- **Where:** src/ui/theme.css:203-206 (`.import-form { display: grid }`, which has no `grid-template-columns`),
  src/ui/theme.css:134-138 (`.settings-panel`, same pattern) and src/ui/ProfileSelect.tsx:36-47 (the `<select>`,
  whose longest option is the 57-character "Auto (calibrated: depth 14 to 18, 0.35 to 0.6 s per move)"). The
  overflow is hidden by src/ui/theme.css:3-7 (`html, body { overflow-x: hidden }`) and src/ui/theme.css:19
  (`.app { overflow-x: clip }`).
- **What is wrong:** the form's implicit grid column is sized `auto`, so it grows to the select's min-content
  width. The measured column is `grid-template-columns: 495px`, inside a form that is only 336 px wide
  (x 12-348). Every child of the form spans x 25-520 on a 360 px viewport: the label text, the textarea, the
  username field, the profile select, the **Analyse** submit button and the engine status line. The right part
  of each is cut off, including the username label "Chess.com username (optional: shows wh…". The Settings
  panel, which also contains `ProfileSelect`, overflows the same way (22 elements reach x 520 when it is open).
  `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` still reports 360 because html and body hide overflow and `.app`
  clips it. As a result, the R30 assertion in e2e/review.spec.ts:357-370 passes while content is unreachable.
  G.29 requires the import screen to fit at 360 to 430 px.
- **Measured:** at 360x780, `.app.scrollWidth` is 520 on the import screen in both themes, and 360 on the
  overview and on move-by-move. Hiding the select brings `.app.scrollWidth` back to 360, which confirms the
  cause.
- **Verify:** at 360x780, open `/` and run
  `document.querySelector('.app').scrollWidth` (expect 360, currently 520). Also check that
  `[data-testid=import-submit]` satisfies `getBoundingClientRect().right <= 360` (currently 520). A screenshot
  shows the textarea and username field running off the right edge.
- **Fix direction:** add `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)` to `.import-form` and `.settings-panel`, or give
  `select` `width: 100%` and a `min-width: 0` grid item. Make the R30 check measure the `.app` scrollWidth or
  element rectangles, so the `overflow-x` mask cannot hide a regression. The test change is the lead's call,
  and it must not weaken the existing assertion.

### H2. Classification-coloured text fails 4.5:1 in the light theme (G.28, review-a11y contrast rule, section 3.6 anti-pattern)

- **Where:** move-list SAN at src/ui/MoveList.tsx:68 (`color: var(--color-classification-<c>)`, with
  "Coloured moves" on by default), tally labels at src/ui/TallyTable.tsx:25, the coach headline at
  src/ui/CoachBox.tsx:65, Retry grade text at src/ui/theme.css:680-691, and the winner's name at
  src/ui/theme.css:411-413 (`best`). The token values are in src/index.css:5-16 (light). Backgrounds are
  `--color-panel #ffffff`, `--color-row-alt #f1f1f1` and `--color-active #e3e2df` (src/index.css:18, 32, 33).
- **What is wrong:** 8 of the 11 classes are below 4.5:1 as text on the white panel. The text is 16-17.6 px at
  weight 700-800, which is below the 18.66 px bold "large text" threshold, so 4.5:1 applies.

  | class (light) | on panel #fff | on row-alt #f1f1f1 | on active #e3e2df |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | brilliant #109888 | 3.58 | 3.17 | 2.76 |
  | great #486688 | 5.95 | 5.26 | 4.59 |
  | best / excellent #5d9948 | 3.43 | 3.04 | 2.65 |
  | good #95b776 | 2.25 | 2.00 | 1.74 |
  | book #8d694b | 4.94 | 4.37 | 3.81 |
  | inaccuracy #e3aa24 | **2.09** | **1.85** | **1.61** |
  | mistake #dd7c2c | 3.00 | 2.66 | 2.32 |
  | miss #ff7769 | 2.59 | 2.30 | 2.00 |
  | blunder #e02828 | 4.66 | 4.13 | 3.60 |
  | forced #96af8b | 2.39 | 2.11 | 1.84 |

  In the browser, computed colours confirm the same values: move SAN for inaccuracy measured 1.85, good 2.00,
  and the active Best row 2.65. The coach headline "Kxg6 is best" measured 3.43. Section 3.6 pins the colour
  values, so the fix is to stop using them as body-text colour in the light theme. Options are SAN in
  `--color-text` with the icon carrying the class, or a darker text-only variant such as a `color-mix` token.
  Record the choice as an Assumption.
- **Verify:** switch to the light theme, finish a review and run the computed-style contrast check on
  `.move-san`, `.tally-label`, `.coach-headline` and `.grade-*` against the nearest opaque background.

## Medium

### M1. Classification-coloured text fails 4.5:1 in the dark theme for Blunder and on the active move row (G.28)

- **Where:** the same elements as H2. Dark tokens are at src/index.css:47-58; `--color-panel #262522`,
  `--color-row-alt #2b2926` and `--color-active #4b4847` are at src/index.css:60, 74, 75. The active row style
  is at src/ui/theme.css:743-745.
- **What is wrong:** blunder `#fa412d` measures 4.28 on the panel and 4.05 on alternate rows. The active ply's
  row background `#4b4847` drops almost every class below 4.5: great 3.10, miss 3.49, best/excellent 3.77,
  forced 3.80, good 4.02, brilliant 4.03, book 4.07 and blunder 2.53. Only inaccuracy (5.64) and mistake (4.62)
  pass. The SAN that fails is the active one, which is the move the user is reading. The browser measured
  `move-san|best|active` at 3.77 and `move-san|blunder` at 4.28.
- **Verify:** in the dark theme, go to a Blunder ply and compare the computed `.move-cell.active .move-san`
  colour with `rgb(75,72,69)`.
- **Fix direction:** use a darker active background, or text-colour SAN on the active cell.

### M2. Dark-theme muted text `#8b8987` is below 4.5:1 on panels and on the page (G.28)

- **Where:** src/index.css:63 (`--color-text-muted: #8b8987` in `.dark`). It is used for body text at
  src/ui/theme.css:229-232 (`.note`, `.hint`, for example "Reviews you open are kept…"), :273-277
  (`.engine-status`), :438-441 (`.stat-label` "Accuracy"), :465-469 (tally headers), :494-497 (phase
  headers), :707-711 (opening name), :728-732 (move numbers), :91-97 (`.link-button` "About / Licenses") and
  :153-157 (footer).
- **What is wrong:** the ratio is 4.40 on `#262522` (panel), 4.16 on row-alt, 3.87 on `#312e2b` (page:
  footer, About button, engine status above the review) and 3.24 on `#3d3a37`. The browser confirmed 3.87 for
  `.engine-status` and the footer `.link-button`, and 4.16 for `.move-num`. Section 3.6 lists `#8b8987` as a
  dark text colour, but G.28 requires at least 4.5:1, so the palette conflicts with G.28. Lightening the
  muted token to about `#9a9896` gives roughly 5.3:1 on the panel and 4.6:1 on the page. Record the change as an
  Assumption.
- **Verify:** compute the contrast of `getComputedStyle(el).color` for `.hint`, `.engine-status` and
  `.footer .link-button` against their backgrounds in the dark theme.

### M3. Keyboard users cannot use the eval graph's click-to-jump (R30 "every interactive element is keyboard reachable", G.9)

- **Where:** src/ui/EvalGraph.tsx:62-76 (`<svg role="img" … onClick onMouseMove>`, with no `tabIndex` and no
  key handler), plus the key-moment ticks and hover tooltip at :100-115 and :160-168.
- **What is wrong:** the graph is interactive. Clicking jumps to a ply and opens move-by-move from the
  overview, and hovering shows the tooltip G.9 requires. None of this is reachable without a mouse. On the
  overview, Tab visits only Start Review, Share and the footer (measured tab order). `role="img"` also makes
  the children presentational.
- **Verify:** on the overview, Tab through the page; the graph never receives focus. With focus on the
  graph, Enter or the arrow keys cannot select a ply.
- **Fix direction:** make the graph focusable with arrow and Enter handling, or add a visually hidden button
  per key moment. The move list already covers the move-by-move screen.

### M4. Board pieces are unnamed, disabled tab stops: up to 32 "button"s in the focus order (R30, focus order)

- **Where:** src/ui/Board.tsx:95-116. react-chessboard 5.12.1 wraps each piece in a dnd-kit draggable
  (`div[tabindex=0][role=button][aria-roledescription=draggable][aria-disabled=true]`) even when
  `allowDragging` is false.
- **What is wrong:** on move-by-move, Tab passes through every piece between the toolbar (Flip, Share) and the
  coach box. That was 7 stops at the final position of the fixture game and 32 in Retry mode. Each stop is a
  `button` with no accessible name, and outside Retry it is `aria-disabled`. The aria snapshot of the board
  group lists 32 `button [disabled]` entries and no names. The focus ring is the browser default
  (`outline: auto 1px`), not the app's `--color-focus` ring.
- **Verify:** on move-by-move, press Tab from the Share button; the focus lands on `div[role=button]` with
  an empty name. `page.locator('[data-testid=board]').ariaSnapshot()` shows the unnamed buttons.
- **Fix direction:** outside Retry, set `tabindex=-1` and `aria-hidden` on the piece wrappers, for example
  through react-chessboard options or an effect. In Retry, give each piece a name such as "White pawn d2".

### M5. Retry mode cannot be used from the keyboard, and the arrow hotkeys break a keyboard drag (R30, G.26)

- **Where:** src/ui/Board.tsx:100 and :111-115 (moves only through `onPieceDrop`), and src/ui/navigation.ts:42-74
  (`handleKey` handles ArrowLeft and ArrowRight on `window` for any non-input target, including the focused
  piece).
- **What is wrong:** in Retry at ply 1, I focused the d2 pawn and pressed Space, ArrowUp, ArrowUp, Space. No
  move registered; the feedback stayed "Play the move you would choose on the board.". I then started a
  keyboard drag on g1 and pressed ArrowLeft. The global hotkey stepped the ply instead: `aria-current` moved
  off ply 1 and Retry exited (`retry` `aria-pressed=false`). There is no click-to-move or other keyboard path
  to enter a retry move, so the Retry feature is mouse or touch only.
- **Verify:** run the Playwright sequence above (scratch script `a11y3.mjs`), or try it manually with only a
  keyboard.
- **Fix direction:** support square click or keyboard move entry in Retry. Make `handleKey` ignore keys while
  a piece is focused or being dragged, or while `retry.active` is set.

### M6. Phase-grade badges expose no class name to assistive technology (R30 "classification badges have aria-labels with the class name", G.14)

- **Where:** src/ui/PhaseGrades.tsx:31-41. The icon is `decorative` (`aria-hidden`), and the label is an
  `aria-label` on a plain `<span>` with no role.
- **What is wrong:** ARIA 1.2 prohibits `aria-label` on the generic role, so browsers drop it. The
  accessibility tree of `.phase-grades` is only `text: Opening Middlegame Endgame White Black`; no grade is
  exposed. The label text G-T3 ("… accuracy 92.7, shown as a move-quality icon.") also never names the class,
  for example "Excellent".
- **Verify:** `await page.locator('.phase-grades').ariaSnapshot()` after a complete review. It should list each
  cell's grade, but currently it lists only the headers.
- **Fix direction:** render the icon non-decoratively (`role="img"`, `aria-label` = class name), or give the
  cell `role="img"` with an `aria-label` that includes `t('class.<grade>')`.

## Low

### L1. The progress status region is created with its first message and removed at completion (R30 "progress announced through role=status", G.4)

- **Where:** src/ui/ProgressBar.tsx:11 (`if (phase !== 'analysing') return null`) and :32 (`<p role="status">`).
- **What is wrong:** a MutationObserver showed the region inserted already holding "Engine: loading". Many
  screen-reader and browser pairs do not announce the initial content of a newly inserted live region, so the
  first message can be lost. The region unmounts as soon as the phase leaves `analysing`; after completion
  there are no `[role=status]` elements, so completion is never announced. G.4's "Checking…" is announced by
  the coach box's own `role=status` (src/ui/CoachBox.tsx:99), not by the progress region the spec names. It is
  still announced, which is why this is low.
- **Verify:** observe `[role=status]` insertions while importing the fixture. It is added with text, and after
  `data-complete=true` the count is 0.
- **Fix direction:** keep an always-mounted, empty `role=status` element in the review shell, write progress
  and completion text into it, and throttle updates (see the optional items).

### L2. Focus is lost to `<body>` on every screen change (focus order)

- **Where:** src/ui/ImportScreen.tsx:55-58 (submit leads to the review), src/ui/Overview.tsx:157-160 (Start
  Review), src/ui/MoveByMove.tsx:97 (Highlights) and src/App.tsx:41 (New game).
- **What is wrong:** each action unmounts the focused button, and nothing moves focus to the new screen.
  `document.activeElement` is `BODY` after the import and after Start Review (measured). Keyboard and
  screen-reader users get no landing point.
- **Verify:** press Enter on Start Review, then check `document.activeElement`.
- **Fix direction:** focus the new screen's `h1` (`tabIndex=-1`) or the board group after the switch.

### L3. Eval-graph phase labels fail contrast in the light theme (G.28, G.9)

- **Where:** src/ui/EvalGraph.tsx:155 (`color: p.colour`) and src/ui/theme.css:547-554 (0.7 rem text on the
  panel).
- **What is wrong:** "Middlegame" `#FFA459` on `#ffffff` measures 1.96 and "Endgame" `#649bf6` measures 2.78.
  The dark theme passes at 7.81 and 5.52. G.9 pins the line colours, so the labels can use `--color-text` with
  a coloured marker.
- **Verify:** in the light theme, compute the contrast of the computed colour of `.eval-graph-phase` against
  `#fff`.

### L4. The player title chip is below 4.5:1 in the dark theme (G.28)

- **Where:** src/ui/theme.css:394-401 (`.player-title`: `#fff` on `var(--color-classification-blunder)`).
- **What is wrong:** white on `#fa412d` measures 3.58 at 0.75 rem bold. The light theme passes with `#e02828`
  at 4.66. This only shows for titled players, for example "GM".
- **Verify:** compute the contrast for a game with a titled player in the dark theme.

## Optional (not requirement failures)

- The progress text changes on every ply (112 polite announcements for the fixture), and the ETA changes the
  string each time. Consider announcing every 10% or every N plies (src/ui/ProgressBar.tsx:32).
- The tooltips G-T1 to G-T5 and the tally row tooltips are `title` attributes only (src/ui/Overview.tsx:61,
  121, 131; src/ui/TallyTable.tsx:21; src/ui/PhaseGrades.tsx:35). They are unavailable to keyboard and touch
  users.
- The key-moments strip uses `aria-label` on a generic `div`, which is ignored (src/ui/CoachBox.tsx:159). Add
  `role="group"`.
- There is no `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` rule anywhere. The eval-bar height transition
  (theme.css:615, still `height 0.25s` under reduce), the progress fill (:330), the key-tick widening (:535) and
  the accuracy counter (`AnimatedNumber`, src/ui/Overview.tsx:18-41) all still animate. The board itself
  complies.
- The single-character shortcuts `f` and `e` are global (WCAG 2.1.4). The spec requires them, and they are
  correctly suppressed in inputs, selects and textareas. A setting to turn them off would fully satisfy 2.1.4.
- The sr-only header of the tally table's middle column reads "Moves" (`movelist.label`,
  src/ui/TallyTable.tsx:14). "Classification" would describe the column better.
- The coach "Prev"/"Next" buttons and the nav "Prev"/"Next" buttons share the same names on one screen. This
  is harmless but redundant.

## Checks that passed (with evidence)

- **G.23 keys:** Home cleared `aria-current` (ply 0); Right twice gave `move-2`; Left gave `move-1`; End gave
  `move-112` with `&ply=112` in the URL. `f` toggled the board label "White at the bottom" to "Black at the
  bottom", and `e` toggled `explain-toggle` `aria-pressed` from true to false. Keys are ignored in
  input, textarea and select (navigation.ts:47) and with modifier keys.
- **First, prev, next and last buttons:** they gave `move-1`… (first leads to ply 0, then next gives
  `move-1`), last gives `move-112`, and prev gives `move-111`. All four have `aria-label`s (MoveByMove.tsx:159-170).
- **`aria-current="true"`:** exactly one at a time, on the active move button (MoveList.tsx:63), which is
  scrolled into view.
- **Badges:** the board badge is `role=img` with `aria-label="Great"` (Board.tsx:62-71). Every non-decorative
  `svg.class-icon` has `aria-label` = class name; on the overview, 0 unlabelled icons were not `aria-hidden`.
  Tally icons are decorative next to a visible label.
- **Eval graph:** `role="img"` with `aria-label="Evaluation graph: from -5.0 to +5.0 over 112 moves"`
  (EvalGraph.tsx:66-67). The eval bar is `role=img` with its value.
- **Error panel:** `role="alert"` with the inline I-34 string, and no modal (ImportScreen.tsx:131). The engine
  error banner is also `role="alert"`.
- **Labelled controls:** the textarea, username field and profile selects use `<label htmlFor>`. The checkboxes
  and calibration inputs are wrapped in labels. The colour and segmented toggles are `role=group` with
  `aria-label` and `aria-pressed`.
- **Visible focus:** every app button, select, input, textarea and link showed `outline: solid 2px` in
  `--color-focus`. That is 6.92:1 against the dark page and 4.77:1 against the light page, above 3:1 for
  non-text contrast. The DOM tab order follows the visual order on all three screens. The board pieces are the
  exception (M4).
- **Reduced motion:** with `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`, no piece transform or transition was sampled over 6
  frames after a step. Without it, the piece moved with `transition 0.18s` (Board.tsx:78-79, 109).
- **360 px:** the overview and move-by-move have no overflow (`.app.scrollWidth` 360) in both themes. The
  board is full width, then the eval strip, coach box, horizontal move strip and graph, per G.29. The import
  screen is H1.
